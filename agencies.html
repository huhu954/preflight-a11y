Why browser-only matters

Client course files can contain unpublished content, student information, or licensed material. The public build parses files in the browser; there is no upload endpoint and no server-side storage. That makes it easier to run a quick preflight without adding another vendor to your data-processing review.

What the report contains

  • A 0–100 preflight score (a sorting aid, not a compliance grade).
  • Findings grouped by critical, serious, moderate, and minor.
  • Location, WCAG reference, plain-English impact, and a fix instruction.
  • A downloadable standalone HTML report you can attach to an internal ticket.
  • A limitations section that states what automation cannot verify.

What it is not

It is not a certification, a legal opinion, or a remediation service. It does not cover PDF, and it cannot judge whether alt text is meaningful or whether reading order makes sense to a person. Use it to triage and scope; keep your manual audit as the evidence layer.
