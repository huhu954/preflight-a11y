    What it checks

    Built for the first pass, not the final sign-off.

    Structure

    Headings, slide titles, duplicate titles, missing document language, text boxes, hidden slides.

    Non-text content

    Missing or generic alt text on pictures, charts, grouped objects, and embedded media.

    Interaction & data

    Ambiguous links, tables without header rows, tiny text, tracked changes, and comments.

    What it cannot do

    It cannot certify WCAG/ADA/EAA compliance, judge whether alt text is meaningful, test screen-reader pronunciation, or remediate the file. A manual review is still required. This is not legal advice.
